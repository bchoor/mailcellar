How it works

Three steps

Each Gmail account or mbox file is a mailbox of its own.

1

Add a mailbox

Sign in to a Gmail account with Google, or upload an mbox file from Google Takeout, Apple Mail or Thunderbird.

2

Backfill copies it

Every month of mail is copied and indexed, and the map shows each one as it lands. New Gmail mail syncs every 6 hours. Copying pauses before a Cloudflare limit.

3

Find anything

Search, sort and group every message by sender, domain, label or period. Open any message, and preview its photos, PDFs and text files.

Where your mail lives

Your Cloudflare account. Nobody else's.

You run MailCellar on your own account. Each user's mail stays in that user's own stores.

Raw files (R2 objects)

Every email as Gmail sent it, attachments included.

One Durable Object per mailbox

The index: one row per message. Every search, sort and group runs here.

Cloudflare Access

Signs you in. Gmail accounts are added separately, each with its own Google sign-in.

Costs

Free tier after the first copy

Real figures from one Gmail account, copied from Nov 2004 to Oct 2026.

First backfill

Workers Free copies about 20,000 messages a day. Workers Paid ($5 a month) lifts that; one month covers the first backfill.

$0

After that

Workers Free is enough. It covers about 20,000 new messages a day.

10 GB

Storage

Raw files include 10 GB a month. Past that, $0.015 per GB-month.
